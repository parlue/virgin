PeSTO piece-square tables

The base shapes of the piece-square tables in src/pst_pesto.h come from the published PeSTO evaluation tables by Ronald Friederich (engine RofChade), as documented at <https://www.chessprogramming.org/PeSTO%27s_Evaluation_Function>. They are evaluation data, not code. Virgin's own Pst.* weights are deltas on top of these tables.
